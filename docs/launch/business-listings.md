## 9. Legal basics (confirm with a CA)
- **Udyam registration** (udyamregistration.gov.in). Free, about 15 minutes with Aadhaar and PAN.
- **GST.** Register when required or when B2B partners ask for it.
- **Ministry of Tourism approval** for tour operators (voluntary; strong trust badge).
- Keep copies of guide licences and driver commercial licences. The website promises both.

When any certificate is issued, send it to me and it goes on the site's Credentials section, which is hidden until then.
