# Earning links (no buying)

A new domain wins links by being genuinely useful and by being listed where travellers already
look. Work down this list once, then keep the last section going monthly.

## Citations and listings (week 1–2)
Use the same name, phone and website everywhere: TajPhotos · +91 89097 41833 · https://tajphotos.com
- Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect
- TripAdvisor (tour operator), GetYourGuide and Viator supplier profiles
- Justdial, IndiaMART, Sulekha
- Incredible India / state tourism directories, if you get Ministry of Tourism approval
- Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest. Pin your own Taj photos linking to the guides.

## Link-worthy assets already on the site
- **Taj Mahal closed on Friday** (`/guides/is-taj-mahal-closed-on-friday`), **Night-viewing dates** (`/guides/taj-mahal-night-viewing-dates`), **Ticket prices** (`/guides/taj-mahal-tickets`): travel writers link to accurate, dated pages like these.
- **The atlas map** on the homepage: real drive times between 7 places.
- **Taj Mahal scams** guide: forums and Reddit threads cite this kind of page.

## Outreach targets (monthly)
1. **Travel bloggers** writing "Golden Triangle itinerary" posts. Offer a correction or an updated fact (ticket prices, Friday closure) with a link to your dated guide.
2. **Wedding and pre-wedding photographers abroad.** Offer a referral partnership for Taj shoots. A page linking to you is a natural result.
3. **University travel and Indian-diaspora associations** (US, UK, Canada). Offer a student or family planning guide.
4. **Hotels and homestays in Agra without their own guide service.** Offer a "recommended local tours" page in exchange for referrals.
5. **Reddit (r/IndiaTravel, r/travel) and TripAdvisor forums.** Answer questions genuinely. Link only when the guide is the best answer.
6. **HARO-style journalist requests** (Qwoted, Featured): offer quotes about Agra and Taj travel.

## Never
Paid "high-DR" link packages, PBNs, link exchanges at scale, or comment spam. Google can wipe a new
domain from search for these, and it is very slow to recover.

## Wikipedia and "Wikipedia-like" sites: what works and what gets you banned
Wikipedia is not a place to upload business information. A page or link added by the business itself
breaks its conflict-of-interest and notability rules, is usually reverted within hours, and can get
the account and the domain flagged. Wikipedia links are also `nofollow`, so they would pass no SEO
value anyway. We do not do this.

What is legitimate, in order of value:
1. **OpenStreetMap** (openstreetmap.org): add the business as a point once there is a real office address. It feeds Apple Maps, Bing and many apps.
2. **Wikivoyage** (en.wikivoyage.org, pages Agra, Delhi, Jaipur): a neutral listing under "Do" or "Buy" is allowed if it states facts only (name, address, phone, website, what it does), carries no promotional language, and is added by someone who has been editing other things. A brand-new account adding only its own listing is reverted. Wait until the business has real reviews, and add the listing from an account that also fixes other entries.
3. **Wikidata**: only with independent references (news, directories), so after launch coverage exists.
4. **Wikipedia, indirectly**: where an article cites a dead official link (for example the old tajmahal.gov.in `.html` pages), anyone can propose the replacement official URL on the talk page. That helps the article, and it is not our link.
5. **Platforms that allow it**: Medium, LinkedIn articles, dev.to and Quora answers with the guide republished or summarised and a canonical link to the original on tajphotos.com; Pinterest boards pointing at the guides; YouTube descriptions.
6. **Directories that are meant for businesses**: Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, TripAdvisor, GetYourGuide/Viator supplier pages, Justdial, IndiaMART, state tourism and Ministry of Tourism approved-operator lists, and the local Agra tour-operator association.

Info to prepare for any listing (copy it exactly everywhere): name, phone, website, one-line description,
service area, categories. The 700-character description is in `business-listings.md`.
